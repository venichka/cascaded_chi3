# Consolidated review of the Optica draft, 2026-10-08

Six independent read-only reviews of `main.tex` and `supplement.tex`: physics, numbers and figures, claims and citations, language, length and compliance, and a simulated Optica referee. Line numbers (M = main.tex, S = supplement.tex) refer to the sources as of 2026-10-08.

## A. Decisions needed

1. **Positioning and novelty.**
   - Resonant cascaded χ² Kerr in a thin, normal-incidence geometry, with an equivalent n₂, was published by **Khurgin & Ding in 1994–97** (co-author's own work, uncited):
     - OL 19, 1016 (1994), 10.1364/OL.19.001016;
     - JOSA B 12, 275 (1995), 10.1364/JOSAB.12.000275;
     - OQE 28, 1617 (1996), 10.1007/BF00331052;
     - Opt. Commun. 139, 63 (1997), 10.1016/S0030-4018(97)00092-8.
   - The single-mode cavity form also appears in:
     - Majumdar & Gerace, PRB 87, 235319 (2013);
     - Gerace & Savona, PRA 89, 031803 (2014);
     - Fryett, Dodson & Majumdar, OE 23, 16246 (2015) (bistability, switching energy);
     - Leo et al. 2016; Hansson et al. 2018;
     - Gustin 2025 (in the bib).
   - LiNbO₃ cavities above 10³× native are published:
     - Musgrave, Nie & Huang, eLight 6, 15 (2026);
     - Nie, Musgrave & Huang, Nat. Commun. 17, 502 (2026);
     - a poled TFLN waveguide reaches 1.6×10⁴× (li2026, in the bib).
   - What remains new:
     - the vector/QNM formulation with η ≤ 1 and the exact bulk limit;
     - the host figure of merit F;
     - the strength–bandwidth identity and the sum rule;
     - the metasurface implementation (no metasurface cascaded n₂, Z-scan or sign-flip work found 2018–26);
     - the SH-only parity protocol;
     - the ring threshold and lock-in analysis.
   - The abstract and introduction must be reframed as a generalisation and a metasurface implementation.
2. **Venue.** The simulated referee recommends **reject at Optica in the planned form**:
   - R ≈ 20 is relative to the host. The absolute |χ_casc| ≈ 6×10⁻²⁰ is below the native χ³ of Si, GaP and AlGaAs, and bulk wins on |χ|Δf.
   - There is no concrete design.
   - The Z-scan signal is ~10⁻⁴ rad at 10 GW/cm² and is unbudgeted.
   - The ring section is incremental.

   It suggests:
   - Optica only with a concrete 3D design, absolute figures of merit and an SNR-budgeted (ideally measured) proposal;
   - otherwise PR Applied (`main_prapplied.tex` exists), Optics Express, ACS Photonics or Nanophotonics;
   - a condensed version for Optics Letters.
3. **Doubly resonant operating regime (Fig. 3c).**
   - With γ₁ = 10γ₂ the SH mode stores 5–10× the pump energy at bistability. The native Kerr cross terms, which Eqs. (1)–(2) omit, are then comparable to the cascade:
     - XPM of the SH on the pump;
     - XPM of the pump on the SH;
     - SPM of the SH.
   - They move the hysteresis window from P ≈ 720–930 to 270–510.
   - Options: add the terms with overlaps ∫|f₁|²|f₂|² and ∫|f₂|⁴, or choose γ₁ ≲ |Δω|.
   - Validity: the cascaded shift must be ≪ |Δω|(R/2)^{1/2}.
4. **Microring R.**
   - Design A uses the fitted g = 1.2 MHz. The measured η_SHG with Eq. (S36) gives 0.56 MHz, so R(A) ≈ 600 and R(B) ≈ 400 instead of 2800 and 1830.
   - Options: quote a range of 4×10²–2.8×10³ in the abstract and Table 1, or justify the fit.
   - Also decide whether to shrink §5 to one paragraph and credit Cui, Musgrave and Nie.
5. **Measurement proposal.**
   - The singly resonant Z-scan needs an absolute detectability and SNR budget:
     - phase per GW/cm²;
     - substrate background (~10–50× the film's native phase);
     - beam waist versus mode nonlocality and SH diffraction orders;
     - a ps source;
     - damage.
   - Alternative: measure the pump-resonance shift and tilt of a doubly resonant sample versus Δω (the metasurface analogue of Cui 2025), which is far more sensitive.
   - Useful exact relations to add:
     - δω/Γ_SH = Δω/γ₂;
     - φ′_max = η_SH,gen(0)/4, which ties the cascaded phase to measurable SHG efficiency.

## B. Physics fixes, independent of the decisions

- **M337–339, switching energy.** 1/(1+R) must include U₂. The total energy scales as (1+R)/(1 + shift/|Δω|); 1/(1+R) holds only for γ₁ ≪ |Δω| (rings). At the Fig. 3 parameters the gain is ~2.2×.
- **M340, bistability penalty.** Write "(1+R)/5 to (1+R)/6 (4.0 at R = 20)" and add the fold-power derivation to S §4.C. Fig. 3(c) is at 1.3× the minimum detuning (ratio 3.5), so do not cite it for the number.
- **M233–236, S377, self-pulsing onset.** The onset shift/|Δω| depends strongly on γ₁ and δ₁: 1.1 (ring, δ₁ = 0), 2.9 (γ₁ = 10γ₂, δ₁ = 0), 9–10 (large δ₁; Fig. 3c upper branch stable up to 1.19× the switch-up power). Give the range.
- **M206–207.** Drop "an undepleted pump" from the assumptions; the CW elimination is exact. The limits are stability and the native cross terms.
- **M228.** With cascaded loss the required detuning is |δ₁| > 1.75γ₁, not √3γ₁/2.
- **Ring operating points (Fig. 5b at 30 µW; S §8.F–G at 20 µW).** These lie above the 8 µW parametric threshold (unstable for δ₁/κ₁ ∈ [−0.65, 2.8] at +2γ₂). Plot and evaluate below ~8 µW, or state it.
- **M444, "cancellation of native Kerr".** Not feasible for design B (needs Δω ≈ 3900γ₂ or Δω ≈ 0); possible only in design C (Δω ≈ 28γ₂). Qualify or drop.
- **M443, "femtojoule switching".** Not computed (estimate ~60 fJ at 37 µW for B). Add the number to S §8 or drop it.
- **R⁽¹⁾ and reciprocity.** The Z-scan measures ∫e₁^rec·δP. R⁽¹⁾ equals the measured ratio only if e₁^rec ∝ e₁* in the material, which a substrate breaks. State this or redefine R⁽¹⁾.
- **M399–400.** SHG efficiency fixes |M̃| only together with the SH radiative rate and the collection efficiency.
- **M208–211, OR+EO background.** It may be ~−5×10⁻²¹ m²/V², comparable to χ_nat (n⁴r₃₃ ≈ 9|χ²_SHG|). It is flat in Δω but shifts the native baseline; quantify it.
- **Fig. 1(d).** Normalise by |Re χ(2γ₂)|, not the signed value.
- **SH diffraction.** For a qBIC at 1.55 µm on silica, the SH is above the first diffraction order; Q₂ is not limited only by absorption and fabrication.
- **Add the bulk-limit check.** S(bulk) with n₂ = 3.9×10⁻¹⁹ reproduces DeSalvo's measured 0.91×10⁻¹⁹ at 1064 nm.

## E. Language (writing rules)

- "Sum rule" is used for two things: the abstract and S25 versus Eq. (5), which follows from |χ||Δω + iγ₂/2| = C.
- **Symbols with two meanings:**
  - U is both stored energy and per-photon shift; rename the energy W_n.
  - Ω is both the modulation offset and the FSR; rename the FSR D₁.
  - E is both the optical field and the dc field; use E_dc.
- **Undefined symbols:** D₂, z₀, κ₁ (main), η_FWM, R_th, Δk at M66, 𝒫, D_lin, P_SH, P_in.
- Figs. 1 and 4, and panels 3(a) and 3(b), are not called out in the text (an Optica requirement).
