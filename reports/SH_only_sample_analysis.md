# SH-only cascaded-χ³ effects — analysis of the 4 LiNbO₃ samples

Effects computed from the designer's measured designs (samples 1–4) through the singly-resonant
model of `notes/Cascaded_chi3_SH_only_note.tex`. Parameter extraction and all consistency checks are in
`data/SH_only_sample_parameters.md`; the physics and figures are generated by
`cascaded_chi3/sample_effects.py` (figures in `cascaded_chi3/figures/samples/`).

**Operating point:** conservative Δω = +2γ₂ (note default). **Material baseline:** |χ⁽²⁾| = 5×10⁻¹¹ m/V,
χ⁽³⁾_native = 2×10⁻²¹ m²/V². **Normalisation:** η_geom = (|overlap|/area)², bridge `C_bridge = 1`
(absolute magnitudes carry an O(1)–O(few) scale uncertainty until the bridge is calibrated — §6).

---

## Headline

1. **The cascade is a material enhancement of a few to ten.** R⁽¹ᴿ⁾ = |χ³_casc|/χ³_native = **S4 10.0 > S3 7.0 > S1 2.0 > S2 1.5**. All four beat native LiNbO₃ Kerr; S3 and S4 by 7–10×. *(R values updated 2026-10-06 for the corrected cascaded prefactor, ×4; see `paper/checks/PREFACTOR.md`. Phase, loss and FWM values are unchanged; SHG values doubled.)*
2. **The reason is the overlap.** The data-derived η_geom = 0.011–0.044 is **2–10× below** the note's assumed ~0.1. χ³_casc ∝ η_geom, so this directly limits every effect.
3. **No single-pass Kerr *device*.** CW phase is sub-nanoradian/µrad; **ps pulses** raise it to ~mrad (10²–10³× more peak intensity, and they escape CW green photorefractive damage) — enough to *measure* the χ³, but still ≪ 0.1 rad. The lost Q₁² build-up can't be recovered by power.
4. **What *is* worth measuring (χ³):** the **self-focusing↔defocusing sign-flip** across Δω = 0 — a scale-robust cascade signature, read out with ps pulses (Z-scan / spectral SPM); broadband FWM as a second probe. **SHG is χ² — out of scope** as a result, used **only to calibrate** the absolute χ³ scale. Bistability, switching, limiting, AM-modulation are **qualitatively dead**.
5. **Sample 4 is the best of the four** (highest overlap and Q); raising the overlap is the single highest-leverage design change.

> **Verification.** All formulas were re-derived from the note and cross-checked two independent ways
> (in `cascaded_chi3/sample_optimal.py` and by an independent reviewer): the χ³_casc lineshape matches the
> dimensionless adiabatic forms of `analytic.py` to 2.5×10⁻¹⁶; the prefactor-free identity
> Re/Im χ³ = −2(Δω/γ₂) holds exactly; units reduce to m²/V² (χ³) and rad (φ_NL); and the sample-4 spot
> value |χ³| = 1.99×10⁻²⁰, R = 9.97 was reproduced by hand (after the 2026-10 prefactor correction; previously 4.98×10⁻²¹, 2.49). **No discrepancies.** The Boyd degeneracy
> factors (3 for SPM/phase, 2× for loss-vs-phase, cubic pump in FWM), the ε₀ cancellation, and the
> squared overlap in η_geom are all correct.

## 1. Cascaded χ³ spectra — sign flip, loss, enhancement

![chi3 spectra](cascaded_chi3/figures/samples/sample_chi3_spectra.png)

- **Re χ³_casc** (left) flips sign through Δω = 0: self-focusing for Δω < 0, self-defocusing for Δω > 0 — the cascade's signature dispersive tunability (note §`sec:sum-1R`), here with the per-sample overlap built in.
- **Im χ³_casc** (middle, two-photon-like loss) does **not** peak at exact resonance. Because the spatial overlap collapses at Δω = 0 (Fano dip in the COMSOL `overlap`, see §2), the loss is suppressed at the line centre and peaks at finite detuning — a data-driven refinement of the note's constant-η Lorentzian.
- **R⁽¹ᴿ⁾** (right) peaks at modest detuning |Δω| ~ 1–2 γ₂ (overlap has grown but the 1/Δω resonant factor has not yet decayed), not at Δω = 0. All four clear the native-Kerr line (R = 1); S4 (red) and S3 (green) by 7–10×.

## 2. Overlap-limited enhancement and bandwidth

![eta and ranking](cascaded_chi3/figures/samples/sample_eta_and_ranking.png)

- **η_geom(Δω)** (left) is the data-derived geometric overlap. All four samples sit **well below the assumed 0.1** at the ±2γ₂ operating points, and dip to ≈0 at exact resonance. The overlap recovers to 0.4–0.8 only far off-resonance, where the 1/Δω enhancement is gone — so there is a finite optimal detuning rather than "sit on resonance."
- **R⁽¹ᴿ⁾ bars** (middle) at Δω = ±2γ₂: S4 10.0, S3 7.0, S1 2.0, S2 1.5. The +/−2γ₂ branches differ slightly (overlap asymmetry) — the focusing and defocusing strengths are not equal.
- **|χ³_casc|·Δf** (right, log) — the bandwidth-integrated nonlinear strength. Even the best sample sits **~10–50× below bulk PPLN** (note's reference; the range reflects the 2–10 THz bulk bandwidth assumed). The metasurface's case is chip-scale form factor and CW-threshold operation, **not** raw integrated strength.

## 3. Feasibility — single-pass Kerr phase and saturable absorption

![feasibility](cascaded_chi3/figures/samples/sample_feasibility.png)

- **CW: nothing observable.** φ_NL ∝ peak intensity; at the CW/ns damage band (1–10 GW/cm²) the best sample reaches only ~10⁻⁴ rad, and CW is anyway capped far lower by green-SH photorefractive damage. Sub-nanoradian at realistic CW intensities.
- **Pulsed (ps) helps — the χ³ becomes measurable.** ps pulses reach **10²–10³× higher peak intensity** *and* escape the CW photorefractive limit (blue band, ~0.1–1 TW/cm²), lifting the phase to **~mrad** and |ΔT/T| to **~10⁻³** — measurable as a χ³ diagnostic, though still **≪ 0.1 rad** (no device; the lost Q₁² build-up can't be recovered by power).

**Time-domain pulsed simulation** (`cascaded_chi3/sample_pulsed.py`). The bullets above are *quasi-static* (CW χ³ at peak intensity). Since the experimental τ_p ~ 1–3 ps is comparable to the SH lifetime 1/γ₂ = 0.9–1.6 ps, we integrated the actual SH-only TCMT, `da₂/dt = (iΔω−γ₂/2)a₂ + iβ(t)` with β(t) ∝ I(t) (FM instantaneous), for a Gaussian ps pulse.

![pulsed reduction](cascaded_chi3/figures/samples/sample_pulsed_reduction.png)

- The **energy-integrated** nonlinear phase is conserved (a sum rule — the SH delivers its full action, spread over ~1/γ₂); the **reduced, Z-scan-relevant (I²-weighted / peak)** phase is the right observable.
- Phase reduction r_φ = φ_pulsed/φ_quasi-static at Δω=2γ₂: τ_p=1 ps → S1/S2 0.80, S3 0.54, **S4 0.41**; **τ_p ≥ 2 ps → 0.9–1.07** (slight resonant overshoot). So the quasi-static ~mrad estimate **holds for τ_p ≳ 2 ps**; the penalty bites only at 1 ps on the highest-Q samples (~2.5× on S4). **Recommendation: use τ_p ≳ 2 ps.**
- The **sign flip survives** pulsing (reduced + spectrally broadened, still flips at Δω=0) — the sign-flip experiment is robust at ps durations.

## 4. FWM and SHG conversion

![fwm and shg](cascaded_chi3/figures/samples/sample_fwm_shg.png)

- **Broadband cascaded FWM** (left): efficiency reaches ~10⁻⁴ only at the damage threshold for the best samples. Its one advantage is that the sub-wavelength thickness makes it **phase-matching-free** (full γ₂ bandwidth) — useful as a *diagnostic* of χ³_casc, not as a device.
- **SHG conversion** (right): a **χ² process — out of scope** as a result of this χ³ study. We use it only because it shares the same overlap and SH resonance, so η_SHG vs detuning/power **calibrates the absolute χ³ scale** (the bridge, §9) and validates the cascade's first (χ²) step. Baseline η_SHG ~10⁻⁷ at 1 GW/cm² (assumes γ_rad = γ₂/2, C_bridge = 1). The χ³ observable itself is the sign flip (§1), read out with ps pulses.

## 6. Which effects are reasonable to see in experiment?

| Effect | Status in SH-only | Observable with these samples? |
|---|---|---|
| **Sign flip (focus↔defocus) vs Δω** | χ³, dispersive shape | **Yes, qualitatively** — scale-robust cascade signature (detuning-resolved Z-scan / spectral SPM, ps pulses). |
| **χ³_casc magnitude / R** | material property | Indirectly, S3/S4 only (R ≈ 1.8–2.5); needs a χ³-sensitive measurement. |
| **Per-photon Kerr phase φ_NL** | single-pass | **Pulsed only** — ~mrad with ps pulses; ≪ 0.1 rad (no device). |
| **Saturable absorption ΔT/T** | single-pass Im χ³ | **Pulsed only** — ~10⁻³ at the ps ceiling. |
| **Broadband FWM** | single-pass | Pulsed diagnostic — phase-matching-free; η ≲ 10⁻⁴. |
| **SHG conversion efficiency** | χ² (out of scope) | **Calibration only** — pins the absolute χ³ scale. |
| Bistability, switching, optical limiter, AM modulator | **die** (no FM cavity) | No, at any power. |

**Measuring the sign flip (the χ³ observable).** Pump at ω₁ (always); scan the pump *wavelength* so its **generated** harmonic 2ω₁ crosses the SH resonance ω₂ (Δω = 2ω₁−ω₂: −→+). A **closed-aperture Z-scan** at each wavelength reads the sign of n₂ from the trace ordering — *valley→peak* = self-focusing (n₂>0), *peak→valley* = defocusing; the cascade is **odd in Δω** and flips at Δω=0. Caveat: native LiNbO₃ Kerr is a constant positive background, so a *full* reversal of the total n₂ needs R>1 (S3/S4); regardless, isolate the cascade as the **antisymmetric-in-Δω** component. Only **~1 nm of pump tuning** spans ±3γ₂ at Q₂≈5000. Equivalent probes: SPM/XPM spectral blue↔red asymmetry, or spectral interferometry for the signed phase.

## 7. Optimal detuning per sample

Scanning Δω over the single-pole validity window |Δω| ∈ [1, 5] γ₂ with the data-derived η_geom(Δω)
(`cascaded_chi3/sample_optimal.py`):

![optimal detuning](cascaded_chi3/figures/samples/sample_optimal_detuning.png)

| Sample | best defocus (Δω>0) | best focus (Δω<0) | R at +2γ₂ | gain over +2γ₂ |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Δω≈+5γ₂, R 2.67 | Δω≈−5γ₂, R 2.21 | 1.98 | ×1.35 |
| 2 | Δω≈+5γ₂, R 2.24 | Δω≈−5γ₂, R 1.94 | 1.54 | ×1.45 |
| 3 | Δω≈+1.9γ₂, R 7.05 | Δω≈−4.3γ₂, R 6.18 | 7.04 | ×1.00 |
| 4 | Δω≈+3.3γ₂, R 10.3 | Δω≈−4.9γ₂, R 10.6 | 9.97 | ×1.04 |

**Detuning is not the lever.** R dips in the |Δω| < γ₂ region (the overlap collapses at exact SH resonance,
and the single-pole model breaks there anyway), and forms a broad, flat plateau in the wings. For the two
useful samples (3, 4) the conservative **Δω = ±2γ₂ is already within ~5 % of the achievable optimum**, with
a slight preference for the focusing branch on S4 (overlap asymmetry). For the weak samples (1, 2) |χ³| is
still rising at the edge of the trusted window, but even there R < 0.7 — they cannot be detuned past native
Kerr. Practical recommendation: **operate at Δω = 2γ₂** (or wherever post-fab trimming lands within
2–4γ₂); the gain from fine-tuning the detuning is marginal, and the real lever is the overlap (§8).

## 8. Design rules for the optimal device

Ordered by leverage:

**R1 — Maximise the nonlinear overlap (dominant lever).** χ³_casc ∝ η_geom = |overlap|², *linear*. The
samples sit at |overlap| ≈ 0.10–0.21 (η ≈ 0.011–0.044), **2–10× below** the η ≈ 0.1 the note assumed.
Targets at S4's Q: |overlap| ≈ 0.21 (η ≈ 0.044, S4 today) gives R ≈ 10; 0.30 (η ≈ 0.09) gives R ≈ 20; 0.42 (η ≈ 0.17) gives R ≈ 40.
**Aim for |overlap| ≳ 0.3 at the operating detuning.** Levers: (a) stronger qBIC asymmetry to lift the SH
mode's spatial average — but respect the η²·Q trade (radiative Q ∝ 1/α²; stay in the absorption-limited
regime where |overlap|²·Q grows with asymmetry, note `sec:eta-compare`); (b) **sub-domain periodic poling**
(unit-cell QPM) to match χ⁽²⁾(r) parity to the SH mode; (c) co-shape the pump near-field E_z(ω)² onto the
SH-mode lobes.

**R2 — Fix the detuning at the conservative point, don't chase it.** §7: the |χ³| plateau is broad and flat
over |Δω| ≈ 2–5γ₂; Δω = 2γ₂ is within ~5 % of optimal for the good samples and keeps the TPA penalty at
(γ₂/2Δω)² = 1/16 and the adiabatic approximation clean. Operating at |Δω| < γ₂ loses (overlap collapse +
model breakdown).

**R3 — Push Q₂, with diminishing returns and a disorder floor.** χ³_casc ∝ Q₂ (at fixed Δω/γ₂); S4
(Q₂ ≈ 5600) is already best. But in 1R only one power of Q helps (no Q₁² FM leverage), bandwidth ∝ 1/Q₂,
and the **array** linewidth must beat fabrication disorder: σ_ω/ω ≲ 1/Q₂ ≈ 2×10⁻⁴ at Q₂ = 5000. Target
Q₂ ~ few×10³–10⁴ and verify the array (not single-element) Q.

**R4 — Thicker LiNbO₃ for the observable.** Single-pass φ_NL, ΔT/T, and FWM all ∝ L_meta (not χ³_casc
itself, which is a material property). Current 180–220 nm; a thicker film raises every single-pass
observable linearly, provided the SH mode and overlap survive.

**R5 — Pump with ps pulses; use MgO-doped LiNbO₃.** The SH is green (488–532 nm), where CW is
photorefractively limited; ps pulses (τ_p ≳ 1/γ₂ ~ ps) give 10²–10³× more peak intensity and escape that
limit, lifting the χ³ signals into the measurable range (~mrad). MgO-doping raises the CW threshold.

**R6 — Confirm Q is the loaded value.** Our FWHM read gives ≈ 0.6× the quoted Q; the model needs the total
γ₂ and χ³_casc ∝ Q₂, so a 1.6× error here moves every absolute number (designer question B3).

**R7 — Know the ceiling: for a CW Kerr *device*, go doubly-resonant.** Even fully overlap-optimised, the
SH-only per-photon phase lacks the Q₁² FM-cavity build-up and stays µrad-class (§3). The SH-only platform's
best roles are (i) a χ³ **diagnostic** (sign-flip + FWM; SHG only to calibrate the scale) and
(ii) **materials-level** χ³ enhancement. A bistability/switching/low-power-Kerr device needs the FM qBIC
added back (doubly-resonant, main note). If the goal is a device rather than a proof-of-mechanism, that is
the decisive design change.

**R8 — Operating wavelength.** The designs cluster at λ₁ = 975–1064 nm; S4 (1064 nm) matches Nd:YAG. Pick
the sample to the available pump, and trim the SH resonance post-fab to set Δω within 2–4γ₂.

## 9. Scale caveats

Absolute magnitudes (χ³_casc, φ_NL, ΔT/T, η_FWM, η_SHG) scale with `C_bridge` (the 2-D-overlap → 3-D-Brillouin
normalisation, baseline 1), with |χ⁽²⁾|² and with 1/χ³_native — all O(1)-to-O(few) at the moment.
The **rankings, sign-flip, lineshapes, and Q/η scalings are robust**; the absolute scale is pinned by one
absolute SHG-efficiency number (one full nonlinear simulation, or the overlap field-norm convention). See
open items in `data/SH_only_sample_parameters.md` §11.
